Dala: the pension that travels with the worker

A retirement vault that a migrant worker owns. Employers, family and foundations can top it up in public. It keeps paying her after she goes home, whatever country she worked in.

Dala means "to carry" in Tagalog.

What happened, and what the program refused

    Refused steps were simulated against the live program on devnet. They show the error the program returned, so no transaction exists for them.

    The rules the program enforces

    1. open_vault: the worker fixes her retirement date and payout schedule when she opens the vault. Nobody can change them afterwards, including Dala.
    2. contribute: anyone can top up. Each top-up is tagged employer, family or other, and records who sent it, so a promised match can be checked.
    3. create_match_pool / fund_pool: a sponsor sets a match rate and a cap per worker, then funds the pool.
    4. save_with_match: only the worker's own deposit triggers a match, and the match is paid in the same transaction. An employer cannot farm the sponsor's money.
    5. claim: from the start date, she withdraws only what has unlocked, one share per period, to her own wallet or to a family member's. There is no other way to take money out.
