Before and after

Twenty years abroad should not end at zero.

Today

  • Host pension schemes shut her out. Singapore's CPF has been closed to foreigners since 2003.
  • Gulf jobs pay a one-time gratuity. It is usually spent soon after she gets home.
  • Moving country means starting again at zero.
  • An employer's promise to help is a private promise.

With Dala

  • One vault under her own key, in every country she works in.
  • Paid out monthly, on a schedule nobody can speed up.
  • Changing job or country changes nothing.
  • Every top-up is public and tagged with who paid.

How it works

Five rules, enforced by code.

  1. 1

    She opens her vault

    With a passkey on her phone. She picks when payouts start and for how long. Nobody can change that later.

  2. 2

    Anyone can top it up

    Employer, family, NGO. Each payment is public and tagged with who sent it.

  3. 3

    Her saving unlocks a match

    A foundation pool matches her own deposits, up to a cap. Only her deposit triggers it.

  4. 4

    The lock holds

    Before her date, every withdrawal is refused by the program. Not by a person who can be pressured.

  5. 5

    It pays her at home

    One share a month, to her wallet or her family's. Whatever country she ends up in.

Questions

Straight answers.

Who holds the money?

She does. The vault belongs to her key. Dala runs the rules for top-ups, matching, locking and payouts, and cannot move her funds.

What if she loses her phone?

Passkeys sync across her devices through her phone's password manager. The plan adds guardian recovery, for example her NGO plus one family member.

Why does this need a blockchain?

No single bank or pension fund works in every country she will live in. A public program gives her one account that crosses borders, makes every match checkable, and enforces a lock nobody can override.

Is this real money?

Not yet. The prototype runs on Solana devnet with a demo stablecoin, and one month lasts 30 seconds so you can see payouts. It has not been audited.

How does Dala make money?

A small yearly fee on balances, employer plans for payroll deduction and matching, and paid matching pilots for foundations. Yield from tokenised Treasury bills would come later through a licensed partner.

Carry it home.

Open a vault, get matched, try to break the lock, and watch a payout arrive. It takes about three minutes.
