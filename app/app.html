The problem

You work for years abroad. You come home with nothing saved.

  • 169 millionpeople work outside their home country (ILO)
  • ClosedSingapore's CPF and Hong Kong's MPF leave most foreign workers out
  • One lump suma Gulf end-of-service payment, usually spent within months

No pension follows you when you change employer or country.

What Dala does

A pension vault in your own name

It lives on your phone, opened with your fingerprint. It stays yours when you change employer or country.

Who pays in

You save. Others add to it.

When you save, a foundation matches it. Your employer and your family can pay in too. Every payment shows who sent it.

Why it lasts

Locked until the day you choose

Then it pays one share each month, to you or your family at home. Before that day, nobody can take it out: not an employer, an agent, or Dala.

How you start

Four steps, about two minutes

  1. Create your walletwith your fingerprint or face. No seed phrase, no bank account.
  2. Open your vaultand pick when monthly payouts begin.
  3. Save a littleand watch the foundation match it.
  4. Send your link to your employerso they can pay in too.

This is a live demo on Solana devnet. You get 500 demo dollars, and Dala pays the network fees. A month lasts 30 seconds here, so you can see payouts happen.
